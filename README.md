Engineering, Management and Technology for Human Well-being.

I’m a civil engineer whose work began with municipal water and wastewater networks in Gaza. I have managed technology services and developed healthcare information systems. In my research, I build tools that let readers trace a reported result back to its source and check the calculation themselves.

I’m an MSc candidate in Civil Engineering – Project Management at the Islamic University of Gaza. I have submitted my thesis and am awaiting defense. My research interests are **project management, disaster management, sustainability and technology**. My thesis examines how households in Gaza can prioritize feasible improvements to shelter and essential services under prolonged disruption.

## Research software and materials

- **gazahomes 0.0.1** is my R package for exploring nine published aggregate indicators. It pairs English and Arabic definitions with the counts and denominators used in the study. The study used a non-probability sample, so the estimates describe the participating households. The released package contains aggregate tables and no participant-level records. [Version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24526526](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24526526) · [Original GitHub release](https://github.com/SulaimanAllouh/gaza-resilient-homes-research/releases/tag/gazahomes-v0.0.1) · [R-universe package](https://sulaimanallouh.r-universe.dev/gazahomes) · [Installation instructions](https://github.com/SulaimanAllouh/sulaimanallouh.r-universe.dev/blob/main/README.md)
- **Gaza Resilient Homes Research 0.1.0** is the separate research companion. It includes the aggregate tables, bilingual dictionary, R scripts and offline reports. [Companion release](https://github.com/SulaimanAllouh/gaza-resilient-homes-research/releases/tag/v0.1.0)
